# Meio Plates

## 1.0.1

- Fixed: a spell cast at your mouseover or focus (macros) drew on your target's nameplate;
  it now goes on the mob it was cast at
- Fixed: Immolate's cast band was missing (its damage over time was read as the whole spell)
- Fixed: a cast that failed without an error message could leave its band on the nameplate
- Works with Meio Shards for the Drain Soul shard cap

## 1.0.0

- First release: on enemy nameplates, the damage still coming from you (the spell being
  cast, each DoT tick by tick), then one of your main attacks, and `kill` or `xN` attacks
  left
- Counts in wand shots, Auto Shots, melee swings or your main spell, whichever fits your
  class; your pet's damage is added on the mob it attacks
- The mob's tooltip lists how many of each attack it still takes; Shift shows each DoT, hit
  sizes and your pet
- DoTs and channels for Warlock, Priest, Druid, Hunter, Mage, Shaman and Warrior
- Crowd control timers for Warlock and Mage; Drain Soul cue for warlocks
- Settings: Esc > Options > AddOns > MeioPlates, also from the addon compartment
- Works with Threat Plates, DoesItDie and Meio DPS
